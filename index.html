World of Warcraft: Commentary Video

For this project, I created a World of Warcraft commentary video focused on a boss fight from the perspective of a Brewmaster Monk tank. I recorded the gameplay and added commentary explaining the fight, including mechanics, strategy, gear, and talent choices. This project gave me experience with recording gameplay, editing a video, and explaining game mechanics to an audience.

Sound to Animation: Project 2

For this project, I removed the original audio from a short animation and created my own version of its sound. I added a different background track and selected sound effects to match the character's movements and events happening on screen. I focused on timing the audio with the animation and creating a different atmosphere for the scene.

Sound to Animation: Project 3

For this project, I reworked the audio for a cinematic from World of Warcraft. I removed the original audio, recorded different voices for three characters, and edited the recordings to match their lip movements. I also added my own sound effects and edited the audio around different cuts in the cinematic. This project allowed me to work with voice recording, sound design, and syncing audio with visuals.

VRChat/IRL Montage Project

For this project, I recorded footage from both VRChat and real-world environments to create a montage connecting virtual and everyday spaces. I edited the clips together with a background song and focused on syncing different cuts and visual moments with the flow of the music. This project gave me more experience with video editing, pacing, and syncing visuals with audio.

Projection Mapping: Project 1

For this project, I created a projection mapping video designed for a circular surface. I recorded and edited in-game footage while keeping the shape and size of the projection area in mind. I focused on positioning the footage so important visuals would remain centered and visible on the circular surface. This project gave me experience with designing video around a physical display instead of a traditional screen.

About Me

My name is Lucas Meinecke, and I am a Game Design student at Illinois State University. I am interested in video editing, sound design, level design, and creating interactive experiences. I especially enjoy editing video and syncing audio with movement, cuts, and other visuals to make scenes feel more immersive. I also enjoy working with environments and thinking about how a player experiences and moves through a space. Through my projects, I have worked with video editing, sound effects, voice recording, projection mapping, interactive concepts, and game design.
