Selected Projects

Gaming / Commentary

World of Warcraft: Commentary Video

In this video, I showcase a boss fight in World of Warcraft on my Brewmaster Monk. I explain how the fight works, what to watch out for while playing as a tank, and discuss gear, talent builds, strategy, and tanking tips.

Sound Design

Sound to Animation: Project 2

I created my own version of a short animation by removing the original audio, adding a different background track, and creating sound effects that match the character and action in the video.

Sound Design / Voice

Sound to Animation: Project 3

I edited a cinematic from World of Warcraft. I recorded different voices for three characters, synced their lip movements with my voice, removed the original audio, and added my own sound effects, recordings, cuts, and edits.

Video Editing

VRChat/IRL Montage Project

I combined in-game VRChat footage with real-world scenery recordings. I used music in the background and edited the clips to sync with the flow of the song while showing visuals from both virtual reality and everyday life.

Projection Mapping / Editing

Backrooms Hallway Edit

I created an edit of a hallway inspired by “The Backrooms” for my Projection Mapping class. I focused on making the scene feel immersive and unsettling through lighting, movement, and editing.

Projection Mapping

Projection Mapping: Project 1

I used a circular-shaped object as my base, recorded and edited in-game footage, and aligned the video so the projection fit inside the circle without being cut off or noticeably off-center.

ABOUT ME

Lucas Meinecke

My name is Lucas Meinecke, and I am a Game Design student. I am interested in creating interactive projects that combine visuals, design, and player experience. I enjoy working on UI, environments, and immersive concepts.

I enjoy editing videos using my own audio, with voice acting and various effects. I also enjoy syncing video to music, like when creating a montage. I also do commentary on certain video games I play.
