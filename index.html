VRChat/IRL Montage Project

For this project, I recorded both in-game footage inside VRChat and in-person scenery footage. My idea for this project was to showcase visuals from both the virtual reality world and our everyday world. About Me

My name is Lucas Meinecke, and I am a Game Design student. I am interested in creating interactive projects that combine visuals, design, and player experience. I enjoy working on UI, environments, and immersive concepts. I enjoy editing videos using my own audio, with voice acting and various effects. I also enjoy syncing video to music, like when creating a montage. I also do commentary on certain video games I enjoy playing.
